Random Myth Defense: Merge TD Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-14 · Revised: 2026-11-12 (ad measurement by TikTok in the Android version — 1.4) · Revised: 2026-11-12 (1.2.0 — ads and in-app purchases)

Summary

  • No sign-up or login. Your progress stays on this device only.
  • We have no servers and receive no information about you.
  • From version 1.2.0 the game has Google AdMob rewarded ads. An ad plays only when you tap a button; there are no banner or interstitial ads. To prepare and deliver ads, Google receives some device information directly (1.5).
  • From version 1.2.0 the game has in-app purchases. Payment is handled by the Apple and Google stores; we never receive your card details (1.6, section 4).
  • Shrine draws are random. Because Ambrosia, which draws cost, can be bought, the odds for every hero are shown at Shrine › "View Odds" before you draw (section 4).
  • On iPhone and iPad, your best scores may be posted to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. Apple handles this; we receive nothing (1.2).
  • We haven't added any analytics or crash-reporting tools. The game engine (Unity) does send anonymous device-spec statistics to Unity (1.3).
  • The Android version includes TikTok's ad measurement tool, which sends the fact of installs, launches and return visits, plus the device's advertising ID, to TikTok. This is to measure whether our TikTok ads lead to installs, and is separate from the ads shown inside the app (1.5) (1.4). The iPhone and iPad version does not include it.
  • A friend challenge code is 12 characters containing only a run number, mode, map, stage and score, and you pass it on yourself (section 6).

  • If it isn't signed in, iOS may show its own sign-in screen. If you don't sign in or you decline, nothing is sent to the leaderboards and you can keep playing with on-device records.

1.6 Handled by Apple and Google — in-app purchases (from version 1.2.0)

When you buy something, payment, billing and refunds are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your name, card number or billing address, and we do not send receipts to any server of ours (we have none). The app talks to the stores through Unity's in-app purchasing library (Unity IAP) and only uses the product name and transaction ID the store provides, on the device (1.1). Payment information is governed by each store's privacy policy.

2. Age and children

The app does not ask your age and has no age screen. It is rated 9+ on the App Store and listed for ages 13 and up on Google Play. We don't receive information directly from anyone, including children. However, other companies' tools built into the app work the same way regardless of age.

  • The engine statistics in 1.3 are sent to Unity regardless of age.
  • The TikTok measurement in 1.4 works the same way regardless of age in the Android version.
  • The ads in 1.5 do not distinguish by age either. For that reason we request only G-rated (general audiences) ad content for everyone, and an ad plays only when you tap a button. On iOS the IDFA is not obtained. On Android the advertising ID may be sent to Google; a parent can delete the advertising ID in device settings (section 9).
  • For purchases by minors, see section 4. Parents can block purchases or require approval with iOS Screen Time (In-App Purchases restrictions, Ask to Buy) or Google Play's purchase authentication and family settings.
  • Parents can decide whether and how a child uses Game Center through iOS Screen Time and Game Center settings.

3. Ads and behavioral data

The only ads in this app are rewarded ads, and they play only when you choose to tap one of the buttons below. There are no banner ads and no interstitial ads (ads that appear without you tapping).

  • Doubling that run's reward on the results screen (once per run)
  • A free draw at the Shrine (once a day)
  • Trying a pack hero you don't own for one run (once a day)

You can take at most 8 ad rewards a day in total. If you buy VIP, you get the same rewards without watching ads. We request only G-rated (general audiences) ad content. You can play the whole game without watching any ads.

4. In-app purchases

  • What we sell: Four items you own for good — the Starter Pack (1,500 Ambrosia plus a Zeus skin; can be bought once), VIP (ad rewards without watching ads), the Norse Myths Pack (6 heroes) and the Olympus Skin Set (3 looks, same powers) — and Ambrosia, an in-game currency added the moment you buy it, in packs of 600, 2,800 and 6,500. Prices are shown in the Shop in your local currency, as provided by the store.
  • Random items (draws): A Shrine draw spends Ambrosia to give you hero cards at random. Ambrosia can be earned in the game or bought. The odds for every hero are shown at Shrine › "View Odds" before you draw. A Myth-tier hero is guaranteed at least once within 30 draws. Norse Myths Pack heroes never appear in draws; they come only with the pack. We do not sell any item where what you get for your money is decided at random at the time of purchase.
  • Purchases by minors: Where local law lets a minor or their guardian cancel a purchase made without the guardian's consent (for example, under 19 in Korea), you can do so. Cancellations and refunds go through the store where you paid.
  • Withdrawal: Ambrosia packs, and the Ambrosia in the Starter Pack, are added the moment you buy them. Ambrosia you have already spent may not be eligible for withdrawal (in Korea, Article 17(2) of the Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce). For the other items (VIP, the Norse Myths Pack, the Olympus Skin Set and the Starter Pack's skin), withdrawal and refunds follow each store's policy and applicable law.
  • Refunds: Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. Google Play: Google Play refund help. We hold no payment information and cannot issue refunds ourselves, but if you email us (section 14) we will explain how.
  • Purchases that need a parent's approval (such as Apple's "Ask to Buy") are not granted until approved.
  • Restore purchases: Use "Restore Purchases" at the bottom of the Shop to get back the items you own for good. Ambrosia exists only on the device and is not restored — if you delete the app or change devices, Ambrosia you bought is lost with it.

5. Leaderboards

The mythological figures on the rankings screen are fixed records built into the game, not real players. The only leaderboards with real players are Game Center on iOS (1.2). There is no free text or chat. To delete your record from a Game Center leaderboard, email us (section 14). We will ask for what we need to identify it (such as the name shown in Game Center and which leaderboard) and delete it with App Store Connect's management tools.

6. Friend challenge codes and the clipboard

  • "Copy Challenge" on the results screen copies a 12-character code to your device's clipboard. The code contains only a run number (which fixes the summon order), mode, map, stage and score, plus one check character to catch typos. It contains no name, device information or identifier.
  • You decide whether and how to pass the code to a friend. The app doesn't send the code anywhere.
  • The clipboard is read only when you tap "Paste" on the challenge entry screen; the app keeps only characters used in codes, up to 12, and discards the rest. iOS may ask you to allow pasting. Codes are entered with a fixed letter pad only; there is no free text input.

7. Vibration, notifications and device permissions

  • Vibration: On iPhone, short haptics play at moments such as merges and bosses. The app reads the iOS "Reduce Motion" setting on the device to tone down screen shake. The Android version does not vibrate.
  • Notifications: The app sends no notifications.
  • Permissions: The app doesn't request camera, microphone, location, photos or contacts. On iOS it does not show the tracking (ATT) prompt. The Android version uses the internet permission to send the engine statistics in 1.3, the TikTok measurement information in 1.4 and the ad information in 1.5, and to talk to the store for purchases (1.6). The Android version has permissions to read the advertising ID (AD_ID — used for 1.4 and 1.5) and the Google Play install referrer (used only for 1.4), and the Google Play billing permission (1.6).

9. Your rights and how to use them

  • Privacy options: In regions where Google's consent tool (UMP) requires it, "Privacy Options" appears at the bottom of the Shop, where you can change your ad consent.
  • Device ad settings: On Android, use "Ads" in device settings (Privacy › Ads, or Google › Ads; menu names vary a little by device) to reset or delete your advertising ID. Once deleted, the advertising ID is sent neither for the TikTok measurement in 1.4 nor for the Google ads in 1.5. On iOS, this app does not use the IDFA.
  • Delete the app: Everything on the device (game progress and records, what you own, transaction IDs) is deleted with the app. Items you own for good can be brought back with "Restore Purchases" after reinstalling, but Ambrosia does not come back (section 4). Scores posted to Game Center remain with Apple even after you delete the app.
  • Game Center: You can sign out or change your visibility in iOS Settings › Game Center. You can ask by email to delete leaderboard records, as in section 5.
  • Access, correction, deletion, restriction: Email us (section 14) and we will act without delay. The only information we hold directly is your email to us; for information held by Unity, Apple, Google or TikTok, we will point you to those companies or act within what we can do (such as deleting leaderboard records).
  • A parent or guardian may exercise these rights on behalf of a child.

10. Retention and deletion

  • Information on the device (1.1): until you delete the app, which destroys it. Only the 64 most recent transaction IDs are kept; older ones are dropped first.
  • Information received by Unity, Apple, Google or TikTok: for the periods in the tables in section 8.
  • Payment records: kept by the Apple and Google stores under their own policies. We do not hold them.
  • Emails to us: deleted without delay once handled, unless the law requires us to keep them, in which case they are kept separately for that period only.
  • Deletion is done so that electronic files cannot be recovered.

11. Security

  • We have no servers and do not collect user information, keeping what we hold to a minimum.
  • The engine statistics, Google ads, TikTok measurement, Game Center and store purchase communications use encrypted connections.
  • Emails to us are managed by our privacy officer.

12. What we don't do

  • No sign-up or login (Game Center uses your Apple account, not an account with us)
  • No banner ads or interstitial ads — the only ads are rewarded ads that play when you tap a button (section 3)
  • The iPhone and iPad version does not use the IDFA or show an ATT prompt (the Android version's advertising ID is used for the TikTok measurement in 1.4 and the Google ads in 1.5)
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools added by us (the engine's device statistics are in 1.3; ad measurement is in 1.4; ads are in 1.5)
  • No company servers · we never receive card details or receipts
  • No location permission, contacts, camera, microphone or photos (the approximate location Google derives from your IP address is in 1.5)
  • No notifications
  • No selling of personal information
  • No free-text input or chat
  • No items where what you get for your money is decided at random at purchase (Shrine draws with Ambrosia, and their odds, are in section 4)
