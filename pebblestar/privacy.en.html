Pebblestar Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-12 · Previous policy (2026-10-08)

Summary

  • No sign-up or login. Your progress and the birth year and month you pick on the age screen stay on this device only.
  • To keep the game free, we show Google AdMob ads. To deliver them, Google receives some device information directly.
  • Personalized ads are shown only to users 18 and over. For users under 14, no advertising ID is sent and personalized ads are off.
  • Purchases are handled by the Apple and Google stores. We never receive your card details.
  • We haven't added any analytics or crash-reporting tools. The game engine (Unity) does send anonymous device-spec statistics to Unity (1.5). Notifications are scheduled on the device.

1.2 Received by Google — ads (Google AdMob, consent via Google UMP)

After the age screen, while you use the app, Google's tools built into the app send the following directly to Google to load and show ads. We do not receive or store this information ourselves.

After you buy "Remove Ads", rewarded-ad buttons give the reward without an ad, but the app may still contact Google to check consent status and prepare ads.

1.3 Handled by Apple and Google — in-app purchases

When you buy something, payment, billing and refunds are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your name, card number or billing address, and we do not send receipts to any server of ours. The app talks to the stores through Unity's in-app purchasing library (Unity IAP) and only uses the product name and transaction ID the store provides, on the device (1.1). Payment information is governed by each store's privacy policy.

1.4 Received by Unity — only if you use the leaderboard (Unity Gaming Services)

The global leaderboard is available only to users 14 and over, and the following goes to Unity only if you use the leaderboard feature. For users under 14 the leaderboard service is never started. Versions without a leaderboard button send nothing.

1.5 Received by Unity — the game engine's device statistics

The engine this game is built with (Unity) sends device-spec statistics to Unity when the app runs. This is built into the engine and we can't turn it off, and we don't receive this information. It does not include your name, contact details, game records or advertising ID.

2. Age screen and users under 14

The app is intended for ages 13 and up. On first launch you pick your birth year and month; nothing is pre-selected. The answer is stored only on the device and is used as follows. Ads and the leaderboard do not start until the age screen is done.

AgeAdsLeaderboard
Under 14No advertising ID sent; personalized ads and remarketing off (Google's child treatment). Only G-rated (general audiences) ad contentOff
14–17Personalized ads and remarketing off (Google's teen treatment). Rated PG or lowerAvailable
18 and overPersonalized ads may be shown depending on consent. Rated PG or lowerAvailable

For users under 14, we do not carry out any processing that would require a parent's or guardian's consent. We collect no behavioral data for personalized ads, the leaderboard stays closed, and we receive nothing directly. To show an ad on screen, however, some of the information in 1.2, such as the IP address, may be sent to Google.

4. In-app purchases

  • We sell only non-consumable items (such as ad removal, permanent boosts and cosmetics) and Time Warps that apply the moment you buy them. The First Supernova Pack applies a 6-hour warp instantly and unlocks a trail look for good. We do not sell stored paid currency or items with random rewards (loot boxes).
  • Purchases by minors: Where local law lets a minor or their guardian cancel a purchase made without the guardian's consent (for example, under 19 in Korea), you can do so. Cancellations and refunds go through the store where you paid.
  • Limits on withdrawal: Items applied the moment you buy them (Time Warp), and the 6-hour warp applied instantly as part of the First Supernova Pack, can't be withdrawn once applied (in Korea, Article 17(2) of the Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce). The shop tells you this before you buy. For other non-consumable items (such as Remove Ads, permanent boosts, Away Time and trail looks), withdrawal and refunds follow each store's policy and applicable law.
  • Refunds: Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. Google Play: Google Play refund help.
  • Purchases that need a parent's approval (such as Apple's "Ask to Buy") are not granted until approved.
  • Use "Restore purchases" in settings to get your non-consumable items back.

5. Leaderboard

Only if you use the leaderboard feature does the information in 1.4 go to Unity. Other players see only your word-combination name, best stage and pebble icon; there is no free text, chat or friends feature. To delete your leaderboard record, email us (section 13). We will ask for what we need to identify it (such as your leaderboard name) and delete it with Unity's admin tools.

6. Notifications

One notification is scheduled on the device for when your away rewards are full. No server is involved. You can decline or turn it off in Settings and keep playing.

8. Your rights and how to use them

  • Privacy options: In regions where Google's consent tool (UMP) requires it, a "Privacy options" button appears in the app's settings, where you can change your ad consent.
  • Device ad settings: On Android, use "Ads" in device settings (Privacy › Ads, or Google › Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID. On iOS, this app does not use the IDFA; you can turn off Apple's personalized ads in Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising.
  • Delete the app: Everything on the device (progress, settings, birth year and month, transaction IDs) is deleted with the app.
  • Access, correction, deletion, restriction: Email us (section 13) and we will act without delay. The only information we hold directly is your email to us; for information held by Google or Unity, we will point you to those companies or act within what we can do (such as deleting leaderboard records).
  • A parent or guardian may exercise these rights on behalf of a child.

9. Retention and deletion

  • Information on the device: until you delete the app, which destroys it.
  • Information received by Google or Unity: for the periods in the table in section 7.
  • Emails to us: deleted without delay once handled, unless the law requires us to keep them, in which case they are kept separately for that period only.
  • Deletion is done so that electronic files cannot be recovered.

10. Security

  • We do not collect user information on our servers, keeping what we hold to a minimum.
  • In-app SDKs send data over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Emails to us are managed by our privacy officer.

11. What we don't do

  • No sign-up or login
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools added by us (the engine's device statistics are described in 1.5)
  • No IDFA (ad tracking); no ATT prompt
  • No precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos
  • No selling of personal information
  • No free-text input or chat
