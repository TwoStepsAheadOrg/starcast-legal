Pebblestar Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-08

Pebblestar does not collect personal information.

No accounts, ads, purchases or analytics. Your progress stays on this device and is deleted when you delete the app.

1. What stays on your device

WhatWhyWhere
Game progress (stage, form, upgrades, gear, attendance, records, space facts learned)To continue where you left offDevice
Settings (sound, shake, reduce motion, notifications)To keep your choicesDevice

We receive no information on any server.

2. Notifications

One notification is scheduled on the device for when your away rewards are full. No server is involved. You can decline or turn it off in Settings and keep playing.

3. What we don't do

  • No sign-up or login
  • No ads, no in-app purchases
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone
  • No sharing, selling or international transfer (there is nothing to send)

The app declares the internet permission because the game engine includes it by default; this version does not send personal information over the internet.

4. Retention and deletion

Everything is on the device, so deleting the app deletes it all.

5. Children

The app is intended for ages 13 and up and does not process personal information off the device.

7. Changes

If we add features that handle information (such as ads or leaderboards), we will update this policy and tell you in the app before releasing that version.
