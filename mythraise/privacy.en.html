Random Myth Idle — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-13

Summary

  • There is no sign-up and no login. Game progress stays on this device only. We run no server and do not receive information about you.
  • The app shows Google AdMob rewarded ads. An ad appears only when you press a button; there are no banners and no interstitials. To prepare and serve ads, Google receives some device information directly (1.3).
  • The app has in-app purchases. Payment is handled by the Apple and Google stores; we do not receive card details (1.4, section 4).
  • The only random draw is one free gear chest a day. It cannot be bought, and its odds are shown in the shop. The four gear items in the Starter Pack have fixed slots and grade; only their alignment is random (section 4).
  • We added no analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own. The game engine (Unity) does send device-specification statistics to Unity (1.2).

After buying VIP, ad buttons grant the reward without an ad, but communication with Google may still occur to check consent and prepare ads.

1.4 What Apple and Google handle — in-app purchases

When you buy a product, payment, billing and refunds are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your name, card number or billing address, and we do not send receipts to a server of ours (we have none). The app uses Unity's in-app purchasing library (Unity IAP) to talk to the store, and uses only the product id and transaction id the store returns, on the device (1.1). Payment information is governed by each store's privacy policy.

2. Age and children

The app does not ask your age and has no age screen. It is not primarily directed at children under 13 and is listed on Google Play for ages 13 and over. We do not receive information directly from anyone, children included. The third-party tools inside the app, however, work the same way regardless of age.

  • The engine statistics in 1.2 are sent to Unity regardless of age.
  • The ads in 1.3 do not distinguish age either. For that reason ad content is requested at rating G for everyone, and ads are shown only when the user presses a button. On iOS the IDFA is not received. On Android the advertising ID may be sent to Google; a parent can delete the advertising ID in device settings (section 7).
  • For purchases by minors see section 4. Parents can block or require approval for a child's purchases with iOS Screen Time (in-app purchase restrictions, Ask to Buy) or Google Play purchase authentication and family settings.

3. Ads and behavioral information

The only ads in this app are rewarded ads, shown only when you choose one of these buttons. There are no banner ads and no interstitial ads.

  • Double the idle income gathered while the app was closed (4 a day)
  • Revive when your hero falls in a run (once per run, 5 a day)
  • Double the faith from a run on the result screen (once per run, 8 a day)
  • The free gear chest in the shop (1 a day)

Ad rewards total at most 15 a day. With VIP you receive the same rewards without watching ads. The game can be played to the end without watching any ad.

4. In-app purchases

  • Products sold: four one-time products — Starter Pack (1,500 faith + four grade-3 gear, can be bought once), VIP Blessing (ad rewards without watching ads), Second Hero Slot, Third Hero Slot — and faith, an in-game currency added the moment you buy it, in amounts of 600, 3,300 and 7,500. Prices are shown in the shop in the local currency reported by the store.
  • Random rewards: only the free gear chest, once a day (by watching an ad, or without an ad for VIP). It cannot be bought, and its odds (grade 2 60%, grade 3 33%, grade 4 7%) are written on its row in the shop. Purchased faith cannot be spent on any draw. Transformation branches and forms are not sold.
  • Starter Pack gear: the four gear items are fixed as one per slot (weapon, head, body, aura), all grade 3. Only the alignment of each item (Order, Might or Chaos) is decided at random, with equal odds (one in three each). The contents of every other paid product are fixed.
  • Cancellation of purchases by minors: under Korean civil law, if a minor (under 19) pays without the consent of a legal guardian, the minor or the guardian may cancel. Cancellation and refunds go through the store where the payment was made.
  • Withdrawal: faith products and the contents of the Starter Pack are added the moment you buy them. Faith already spent may be excluded from withdrawal under Article 17(2) of the Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce. Withdrawal and refunds for the other products (VIP Blessing, hero slots) follow each store's policy and applicable law.
  • Refunds: request them from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com or from Google Play via Google Play refund help. We hold no payment information and cannot refund directly, but if you write to the email in section 12 we will point you to the right place.
  • Purchases that need a guardian's approval (such as Apple's Ask to Buy) are not granted until approved.
  • Restoring purchases: "Restore purchases" at the bottom of the shop gives back one-time products (the contents of the Starter Pack are granted only once). Faith exists only on the device and is not restored — if you delete the app or change devices, purchased faith is lost with it.

5. Vibration, notifications, device permissions

  • The app uses no vibration and sends no notifications.
  • It does not request camera, microphone, location, photos or contacts. On iOS it does not show the tracking permission (ATT) prompt.
  • The Android version uses the internet and network-state permissions to send the engine statistics in 1.2 and the ad information in 1.3 and to talk to the store for purchases (1.4). The permission to read the advertising ID (AD_ID) and the permissions added by the ads SDK (ad services, wake lock, foreground service) are used for 1.3; the Google Play billing permission is used for 1.4.

7. Your rights and how to use them

  • Privacy options: in regions where Google's consent tool (UMP) requires it, "Privacy options" appears at the bottom of the shop, where you can change your ad consent.
  • Device ad settings: on Android you can reset or delete the advertising ID under "Ads" in device settings (Privacy › Ads or Google › Ads; menu names differ slightly by device). On iOS this app does not use the IDFA.
  • Deleting the app: all information on the device (progress, product ownership, transaction ids) is erased when you delete the app. One-time products can be recovered with "Restore purchases" after reinstalling, but faith does not come back (section 4).
  • Requests to access, correct, delete or stop processing: write to the email in section 12 and we will respond without delay. The only information we hold ourselves is the email you send us; for information held by Unity, Google or Apple we will direct you to each company.
  • A legal guardian may exercise these rights on behalf of a child.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Information on the device (1.1): until the app is deleted. Only the 64 most recent transaction ids are kept; older ones are removed first.
  • Information received by Unity and Google: as in the table in section 6.
  • Payment records: kept by the Apple and Google stores under their own policies. We do not hold them.
  • Inquiry emails: deleted without delay once handled, unless a law requires them to be kept, and then only for that period.
  • Electronic files are destroyed in a way that cannot be recovered.

9. Security measures

  • We run no server and collect no user information, keeping what we hold to a minimum.
  • Engine statistics, Google ads and store payment traffic use encrypted connections.
  • Inquiry emails are managed by the privacy officer.

10. What we do not do

  • No sign-up or login
  • No banner or interstitial ads — only rewarded ads shown when the user presses a button (section 3)
  • The iPhone and iPad version does not use the IDFA and makes no ATT request
  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools added by us (engine device statistics are in 1.2, ads in 1.3)
  • No company server · we receive no card details and no receipts
  • No use of location permission, contacts, camera, microphone or photos (the approximate location Google derives from the IP address is in 1.3)
  • No notifications · no sale of personal information
  • No free text input, chat, online leaderboards or matches against other users (Shadow Tower opponents are built into the game)
  • No paid draws. Among paid products the only random element is the alignment of the Starter Pack gear (section 4)
